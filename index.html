Tap Spin or the wheel.

All rules

  • Backwards pool: Your colours become the cue ball. Strike one of your colours instead of the white; it must hit the white before anything is potted.

  • No chalk: No chalking. Polish your tip instead. Opponent may help

  • FULL POWER: Hit the cue ball as hard as you can. Holding back is a foul.

  • Opposite hand: Play with your other hand.

  • Resurrection: Before your shot, your opponent puts one of your potted balls back on the table, anywhere they like.

  • Swap sides: Swap colours with your opponent. If you were reds, you’re now yellows.

  • One handed: No bridge hand. You can use the rest, but you can’t hold it.

  • Spin again and stack: Spin again. The number on the rim where your first re-spin lands is how many more spins you do. All the rules stack.

  • ± Cue ball: Place an extra cue ball anywhere behind the baulk line. If there are already two on the table, your opponent removes one.

  • Snookered: Your opponent can move any ball on the table, as long as it leaves you snookered.

  • Pyramid: Pot the white along with your object ball. On your next turn, place the white anywhere. Potting with no pyramid is a foul.

  • Spin on the clock: Look at a clock, strike the cue ball wherever the second hand is pointing, as if the ball were the clock face.

  • Full recovery: All your balls go back on the table, spaced evenly along the long centre line.

  • Castration: All your balls come off the table. You’re now on the 8 ball.
